Your property brief

The property brief is prepared in your browser. This website does not submit it to a database or send it automatically. When you choose WhatsApp or email, your preferences are included in a draft in that service. You decide whether to send it.

Contacting Tutmey

If you send an enquiry, Tutmey receives the information you choose to share, including the contact information provided by your messaging or email account. That information is used to respond and discuss your property requirements. For questions about an enquiry or how it is handled, contact info@tutmey.com.

Cookies and browser storage

This version of the website does not set analytics or advertising cookies and does not save your property brief to browser storage. Preferences entered in the form remain in the current page until you leave or reload it.

Hosting and external services

The site is designed for GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information, such as an IP address, when serving the website. WhatsApp, your email provider, Google Maps and other external sites apply their own terms and privacy policies when you use them.
